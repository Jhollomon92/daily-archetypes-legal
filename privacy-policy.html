Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026

Daily Archetypes ("the App") is developed by Jerome Hollomon. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Daily Archetypes does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All journal entries and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

What We Don't Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account information
  • No location data
  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with third parties

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management and billing are handled by Apple per their standard terms.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual journal entries within the app. To delete all app data, uninstall the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

Contact
